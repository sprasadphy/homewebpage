Core Research Pillars

Bridging phenomenology and experimental collider analysis across relativistic heavy-ion physics.

Anisotropy & Geometry

Anisotropic Flow & Initial-State Fluctuations

Quantifying harmonic flow coefficients ($v_2, v_3, v_4$) and multi-particle cumulants to map initial geometric eccentricity fluctuations into final-state momentum azimuthal anisotropy in heavy-ion collisions.

Topology & Classifiers

Event-Shape Engineering & Topology

Developing topological classifiers including transverse spherocity and flattenicity to isolate soft-QCD multi-parton interactions, investigate strangeness enhancement, and select specific initial collision geometries.

Artificial Intelligence in HEP

Machine Learning for Collider Observables

Designing deep neural networks and physics-informed models for inclusive, prompt, and non-prompt heavy-flavor hadron identification, as well as DNN-based estimators for elliptic flow coefficients.

Nuclear Structure at LHC

Exotic Alpha-Clustering in Light Nuclei

Probing tetrahedral and exotic α-cluster geometry profiles in $^{16}\text{O}$-$^{16}\text{O}$ and $^{20}\text{Ne}$ systems at TeV energies through flow fluctuations and hydrodynamic modeling (CGC+Hydro, AMPT).
