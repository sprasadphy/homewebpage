Dr. Suraj Prasad

Postdoctoral Researcher

HUN-REN Wigner Research Centre for Physics, Budapest, Hungary

High-Energy Nuclear Physics | ALICE Collaboration

Experimental high-energy physicist studying quark-gluon plasma signatures in small to heavy-ion collision systems. My work spans anisotropic flow coefficients vn, event-shape engineering, machine-learning-driven heavy-flavor identification, and jet/correlation observables at RHIC and LHC energies.

Current Research Themes

  • Event-shape topology and global observables in heavy-ion collisions, including transverse spherocity studies.
  • Initial-state geometry and final-state anisotropy mapping through differential and fluctuation-based $v_n$ analyses.
  • Machine-learning methods for inclusive, prompt, and non-prompt charm/ charmonium separation in proton-proton collisions.
  • Alpha-clustered nuclear geometry effects in O-O and p-O systems at LHC energies.
