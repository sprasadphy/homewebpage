Experimental high-energy nuclear physics

Suraj Prasad

Postdoctoral Researcher, HUN-REN Wigner Research Centre for Physics, Budapest
Member of the ALICE Collaboration at CERN

I am an experimental high-energy nuclear physicist. I work at the HUN-REN Wigner Research Centre for Physics with Dr. Gergely Gábor Barnaföldi, and I did my PhD at IIT Indore with Prof. Raghunath Sahoo.

My research is on the strongly interacting matter produced in proton-proton, light-ion and heavy-ion collisions at the LHC and RHIC. I want to understand where quark-gluon plasma like effects start to appear as we go from small to large collision systems. For this I mainly use event-shape engineering, multiparticle correlations and machine learning, both in phenomenology studies with Monte Carlo models and in ALICE data analysis.

At present, most of my work is on the light-ion collisions at the LHC (pO, OO and Ne–Ne). Here the shape of the colliding nuclei, including the possible α-clustered structure of 16O and 20Ne, leaves its imprint on the anisotropic flow, and I am trying to find observables that can separate it from the other effects.
